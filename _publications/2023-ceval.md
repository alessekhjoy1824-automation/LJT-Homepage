C-Eval is a large-scale Chinese evaluation suite with 13,948 multiple-choice questions across 52 subjects, from different education levels (high school, college, and advanced professional) and disciplines, providing a standard benchmark to evaluate the knowledge, reasoning, and application capabilities of Chinese foundation models.
