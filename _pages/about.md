## About Me
Hello! I'm **Junteng Liu** (nickname: LJT), a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://www.ust.hk/), supervised by Professor Junxian He. I received my Bachelor's degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with a particular interest in large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs).

## Academic Background
- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024 - Present
  - Research group: HKUST NLP Group, led by Professor Junxian He
- **B.Eng.** (Computer Science), Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), 2020 - 2024
  - Graduated June 2024, received Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship

## Research Experience

## Research Interests
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Studying how to improve LLM reasoning capabilities through scalable data synthesis and reinforcement learning
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Analyzing and mitigating perception bottlenecks in VLMs for complex tasks like chart understanding
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Exploring inner representations of LLMs to understand and improve their truthfulness, and developing interpretability methods for better model transparency
